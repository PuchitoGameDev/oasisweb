O.A.S.I.S.

The AI assistant that lives on your PC.

O.A.S.I.S. is an independent project from Galicia, Spain. It is a free, local-first AI assistant for Windows: the models run on your own computer, and nothing you ask it has to leave it.

About

An independent project, not a company.

There is no company behind O.A.S.I.S. and no sales team. The work is done in Galicia, Spain, and released in the open. What that buys is stated plainly on the product site rather than implied here.

Runs on your PC

Compatible local models through GGUF, on hardware you already own.

Free, no account

No sign-up, no subscription and no telemetry. Nothing is collected because nothing is sent.

Still in beta

The honest limits and the roadmap are published, so you can judge it before installing.
