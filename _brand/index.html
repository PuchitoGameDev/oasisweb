The oasis is on your own PC.

O.A.S.I.S. is a local-first AI assistant for Windows. The models run on your own computer, so it keeps working when the network does not, and what you ask it never has to leave it.

The project

An independent project, not a company.

O.A.S.I.S. is built by an independent project. There is no company behind it, no sales team and nothing to buy except a subscription that does not exist yet. The work is released in the open, with the roadmap and the limits published alongside it. We do not publish where the project comes from: a product that asks you to keep your data on your own machine should not start by handing over its author's address.

What this page does not do.

Listed here because a privacy claim is only worth what a reader can check. All three are enforced by the page, not promised by it.

  • No cookies Nothing is kept on your device but your light or dark choice
  • No third-party requests Every byte comes from this site, and the rule that says so is in the source
  • No analytics The only script here changes the theme, and it tells nobody anything
