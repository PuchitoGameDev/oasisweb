O.A.S.I.S.

The oasis is on your own PC.

O.A.S.I.S. is an independent project from Galicia, Spain. It is a free, local-first AI assistant for Windows: the models run on your own hardware, so the assistant keeps working when the network does not, and nothing about what you ask it has to leave your computer.
