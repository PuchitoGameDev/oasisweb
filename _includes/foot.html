Every Jekyll-rendered page includes this, so the blog, the glossary, the posts and the legal pages cannot drift away from the product pages: the site used to have four different footers, and the only reason this file exists is to make a fifth impossible. Paths go through relative_url rather than staying relative, because the pages that use this include sit at different depths (the Journal posts are two levels down) and a relative "download.html" would resolve to /blog/2026/download.html. lp is the language prefix: empty for English, /es for Spanish. The pages under /assets/, llms.txt and the feeds are shared and deliberately carry no prefix. The static pages cannot include this, because a file with no front matter is copied by Jekyll without rendering Liquid.
